# CUM - Couple's Ultimate Manager 💑

**CUM (Couple's Ultimate Manager)** is a small, user-first app for couples to share watchlists, track what they’ve watched together, and remember their thoughts and reviews.

## Features 
👉 **Create a pair.** <br>
Start by creating your own profile. Pair up with your partner, or simply enter their nickname and manage the app on your own.

👉 **Add movies to the shared watchlist.** <br> 
Search an open database for movies or series you want to watch someday and add them to a watchlist shared with your partner. 

👉 **Ratings & Reviews.** <br>
Once you finish watching something from your watchlist, you can mark it as watched and leave a rating and review for yourself and for your partner.

👉 **Stats. And stats. <br>
And even more stats.** We collect your ratings, tags, and genres to create a beautiful reflection of your cinephile soul. Explore a variety of statistics and discover the tiny details behind your picks.

## 🔧 Technologies used
Frontend with **React**, backend with **Supabase**.

**PWA migrated to Capacitor.** Initially built as a Progressive Web App but later migrated to Capacitor due to performance limitations.
